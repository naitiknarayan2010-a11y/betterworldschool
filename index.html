About Us

A Legacy of Learning & Character

Better World School was founded with a single vision: to provide world-class education that develops not just academic excellence, but strong moral character and critical thinking in every student.

Over one decade, we have grown into a premier institution trusted by thousands of families, producing graduates who contribute meaningfully to society and lead with integrity.

  • CBSE affiliated curriculum with enriched co-curricular activities
  • Experienced and dedicated teaching staff with ongoing training
  • Safe, inclusive, and growth-oriented campus environment
  • Focus on both academic performance and personal development
  • Digital learning tools integrated with traditional pedagogy

Admission Process

Simple steps to secure your child's future at Better World School.

  • 1
    Enquiry & Form Collection
    Visit the school office or use the enquiry form online. Collect the admission application form.
  • 2
    Document Submission
    Submit birth certificate, previous school reports, Aadhaar card, and passport-size photos.
  • 3
    Interaction & Assessment
    A brief interaction for the student and parents with the principal. No formal entrance exam for Classes 1–8.
  • 4
    Confirmation & Fee Payment
    Receive the admission confirmation letter and complete fee payment to secure the seat.
