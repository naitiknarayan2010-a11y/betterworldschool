Privacy Policy

Better World School app · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Better World School app and website (the “App”) collects, why, and how it is handled. The App is run by Better World School, Shanti Market, Kumhrar, Patna – 800026, Bihar, India.

Who uses the App

The App is used by students, parents/guardians, teachers and school staff. Accounts are created by the school office. Visitors can also read general school information and send an enquiry without logging in.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, username, class, roll number and password set by the school.
  • School records: attendance, homework, test results, exam attempts, fee records and payment confirmations, leave requests, doubts and notifications.
  • Contact details: parent/guardian name and phone number, and the name, contact number and message you type into the enquiry or “forgot password” form.
  • Technical data: a sign-in session stored on your device so you stay logged in, and cached files so the App loads faster.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data automatically.

How we use it

Only to run school services: showing students and parents their own records, letting teachers manage classes, sending school notices, and answering enquiries. We do not show advertisements, and we never sell personal information.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), which processes it on our behalf under Google’s security and privacy terms. Access is limited to signed-in users of the App; school staff can view the records needed for their work. We do not share personal information with third parties, except when required by law.

Children

The App is intended for school use. Student accounts are created and managed by the school, and parents/guardians are welcome to contact us at any time about their child’s information.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while the student is enrolled and as the school is required to keep them. To have an account and its data corrected or deleted, email info@betterworldschool.edu.in or call +91 88775 24769 (Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM). Requests are handled by the school office.

Security

Connections use HTTPS and data is held in Google’s secure infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell the school if you think someone else knows it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
